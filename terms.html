Terms of Service

Service scope

Japan Insider provides travel research, comparison, information, consultation and concierge-style guidance. Unless explicitly changed in writing, Japan Insider does not sell or contract hotel rooms, transportation, package tours or other third-party travel products on the customer's behalf.

Quick Help

Quick Help is an asynchronous email service. After payment, the customer submits up to three focused questions through the intake form. No appointment or video call is required. Answers are usually delivered within 2 business days after the required information is received.

Personal / Premium / VIP

The scope, delivery timing and any support window are confirmed before payment. Premium's 7-day support is not an emergency service and does not guarantee immediate responses or third-party availability.

Third-party services

Hotels, restaurants, transport operators and other third parties set their own prices, availability and terms. Customers make bookings and payments directly with those providers .

Refunds

A full refund is available before individualized research begins. Once individualized research has begun, customer-requested refunds are generally unavailable due to the customized nature of the service. If Japan Insider cannot provide an agreed service, the unprovided portion will be refunded or an appropriate alternative offered.

No emergency service

Japan Insider is not an emergency, medical, legal, immigration, police or disaster-response service. Customers should contact the appropriate official service in emergencies.

When individualized research begins

Individualized research begins when Japan Insider sends the customer a work-start confirmation email. Refund eligibility tied to the start of research is determined using that written confirmation.

Travel-provider transactions

Japan Insider does not book, purchase, resell or contract with travel providers on the customer’s behalf. Customers make and pay for reservations directly with each provider.

Revision requests

A revision means reasonable adjustments based on the original trip dates, destinations and stated preferences. Requests must be submitted within 7 calendar days of delivery. Material changes to dates, destinations, trip length or original scope may require a new quote or additional fee.

Terms of Service

Service scope

Japan Insider provides travel research, comparisons, information, recommendations and booking guidance. Japan Insider does not book, purchase, resell, or contract with hotels, transportation providers, restaurants or other travel providers on the customer’s behalf. Customers make, contract and pay for third-party services directly with each provider.

Quick Help

Quick Help is an asynchronous service delivered by email. After payment, the customer submits up to three focused Japan travel questions through the intake form. No appointment or video call is required. Answers are usually delivered within 2 business days after the information needed to begin has been received.

Personal Plan

Personal Plan is designed for trips of up to 7 days and 2 main cities or regions, with a target delivery of 5 business days after the required information is received. It includes 1 reasonable revision.

Premium

Premium is designed for trips of up to 14 days and 4 main cities or regions, with a target delivery of 7 business days after the required information is received. It includes 2 reasonable revisions and one 7-day email support window. Support hours are 09:00–18:00 JST, with a typical response target within 4 hours during support hours. It is not an emergency service and does not guarantee immediate responses or third-party availability.

VIP

VIP scope, delivery timing and support are defined in a written quote before payment. The launch payment model is 100% upfront after the customer accepts the written scope and quote.

When individualized research begins

Individualized research begins when Japan Insider sends the customer a work-start confirmation email. Refund eligibility tied to the start of research is determined using that written confirmation.

Refunds

A full refund may be requested before individualized research begins. Once individualized research has begun, customer-requested refunds are generally unavailable because the service is customized. If Japan Insider cannot provide an agreed service, the unprovided portion will be refunded or an appropriate alternative will be offered, subject to applicable law.

Revisions

A revision means reasonable changes within the original trip dates, destinations and stated preferences. Revision requests must be submitted within 7 calendar days of delivery. Material changes to dates, destinations, trip length or the original scope may require a new quote or additional fee.

Third-party services

Hotels, restaurants, transport operators and other third parties set their own prices, availability, rules and terms. Recommendations and availability information can change, and customers are responsible for reviewing the provider’s current terms before booking or paying.

No emergency service

Japan Insider is not an emergency, medical, legal, immigration, police or disaster-response service. Customers should contact the appropriate official service in emergencies.

サービス範囲

Japan Insiderは、旅行に関する調査、比較、情報提供、おすすめの提案、予約方法の案内を行います。ホテル、交通機関、飲食店その他の旅行関連事業者について、お客様に代わって予約、購入、再販売、契約を行いません。第三者サービスの予約・契約・支払いは、お客様ご自身で各事業者に直接行っていただきます。

Quick Help

Quick Helpは非同期のメール回答型サービスです。決済後、専用フォームから日本旅行に関する集中質問を最大3つ送信していただきます。予約やビデオ通話は不要です。開始に必要な情報を受領後、通常2営業日以内を目安にメールで回答します。

Personal Plan

Personal Planは、最大7日間・主要2都市／地域までの旅行を基本範囲とし、必要情報の受領後5営業日以内を納品目安とします。合理的な修正1回を含みます。

Premium

Premiumは、最大14日間・主要4都市／地域までの旅行を基本範囲とし、必要情報の受領後7営業日以内を納品目安とします。合理的な修正2回と、7日間のメールサポート1回を含みます。サポート時間は日本時間9:00〜18:00で、時間内は通常4時間以内の返信を目安とします。緊急サービスではなく、即時返信や第三者サービスの空き状況を保証するものではありません。

VIP

VIPの具体的な範囲、納期、サポート内容は支払い前の書面による見積で定めます。開始時の支払条件は、書面の範囲と見積を承諾後、100%前払いです。

Japan Insiderが「作業開始確認メール」をお客様へ送信した時点で、個別リサーチ開始とします。リサーチ開始に連動する返金可否は、この書面による確認を基準に判断します。

返金

修正

修正とは、当初の旅行日程、訪問先、希望条件の範囲内で行う合理的な変更を指します。修正依頼は納品から7暦日以内にお願いします。日程、訪問先、旅行日数、当初の依頼範囲を大きく変更する場合は、新たな見積または追加料金が必要になることがあります。

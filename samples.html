QUICK HELP — SAMPLE DELIVERY

Three questions. Three usable answers.

Illustrative sample. Transport times, fares and booking details are rechecked for the customer's actual travel date.

1. Should we stay in Shinjuku or Shibuya?

Recommendation: Shinjuku. For this couple's priorities—easy transportation, comfortable full-service hotels and a choice between lively and quieter areas—Shinjuku is the better fit. Shibuya is excellent for nightlife, fashion and a younger street atmosphere, but the station area can feel more hectic. Choose Shibuya instead if nightlife, contemporary shopping and proximity to Daikanyama/Harajuku matter more than the broadest transport options.

2. What is the simplest way from Haneda Airport to Shinjuku?

For comfort with luggage: first check an airport limousine bus serving your hotel or a nearby major hotel; it can avoid carrying bags through large stations. For speed and flexibility: rail is usually straightforward, but the best route depends on terminal, arrival time and hotel location. For a paid answer we verify the practical routes, closest stop, current timetable, fare and luggage convenience for your actual date.

3. Four nights Tokyo + four nights Kyoto — sensible?

Yes, for a first eight-night trip. It gives enough time to experience both cities without changing hotels too often. We would group sightseeing geographically rather than crossing each city repeatedly. If food and neighborhoods matter more than checklist sightseeing, keeping all four Kyoto nights is often better than adding another one-night stop. If your international flight departs from Tokyo, we may recommend returning to Tokyo the night before departure when that materially reduces travel-day risk.

What a paid answer adds

Your actual delivery is tailored to your dates, hotel area, pace and priorities. Where relevant, it includes named options, trade-offs, approximate travel time or price range, booking instructions, alternatives, the date information was checked, and source links.
